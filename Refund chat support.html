FAQs

Quick answers

Find answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find it? Our chat team is one click away.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships you'll receive an email with a tracking link. You can also view live tracking any time from Account → Orders. If your tracking hasn't updated in 48 hours, start a live chat and we'll chase it for you.

What is your return & refund policy?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they're unused and in their original packaging. Some exclusions apply (personalised items and hygiene products). Start a return from your order page or ask us in chat.

How long does a refund take?

Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 2 business days and usually appear on your original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

I can't log in to my account — what should I do?

Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page and we'll email a reset link within a minute. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive. If you're still locked out, open a live chat and we'll verify your identity and restore access.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes — if your order hasn't shipped yet, you can update the address from Account → Orders → Edit delivery. If it's already on its way, chat with us and we'll contact the courier on your behalf.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Go to Account → Subscription → Manage plan. You can pause, downgrade, or cancel at any time — no fees, no phone call required. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All payments are processed by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified providers over 256-bit TLS encryption. We never store your full card number on our servers.

Do you offer support in other languages?

Yes. Live chat is available in English, Spanish, French, German, and Hindi. Just let us know your preferred language at the start of the chat and we'll route you to the right agent.
